Live Age of Empires II: DE lobby browser

AoE2 Lobby Observer shows every open multiplayer lobby in Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition as it happens: title, map, game mode, players, civilizations, teams and the lobby's average Elo. Filter by exact map or Elo range, search for a player, and join in one click. It is a free, open-source successor to the aoe2lobby.com lobby browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I join a lobby?

Click Join. Your game opens directly in that lobby (the link looks like aoe2de://0/518271971). Age of Empires II: DE must be installed on the same PC. Watch appears when a lobby allows spectators. The copy button copies the same join link, for example to share on Discord.

What does the Elo column show?

The average rating of the players in the lobby: team Random Map rating for team lobbies and 1v1 rating for 1v1 lobbies. Under Filters you can switch to 1v1, team or Empire Wars ratings and set a minimum or maximum Elo. Hover a player's name in an opened lobby to see their 1v1 and team ratings.

How does the team balance suggestion work?

Open a lobby with 4 to 8 players to see the most even teams you can reach by changing at most 4 players, 2 per team. Each player's rating is the average of their 1v1 and team rating, ignoring ladders with fewer than 40 games. Experienced players are balanced first, then players with few games, then unrated players, and the highest and lowest rated before the rest. Copy balance suggestion copies one line to paste into the lobby chat.

Where does the data come from?

From the official Age of Empires II: DE game servers, the same lobby list the game shows, refreshed every 5 seconds. Ratings come from the official leaderboards. The data is also available as plain text and JSON.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source on GitHub. Suggestions and pull requests are welcome.
